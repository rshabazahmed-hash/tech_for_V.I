typing_manager

typing_manager is a Windows application for practical typing and dictation practice, with strong accessibility support for NVDA screen reader users. Type what you hear and improve your speed and accuracy.

Key features

  • NVDA speech support for accessible, speech-based dictation practice.
  • Adjustable dictation speed from 7 to 30 WPM.
  • Custom practice text: enter or paste your own content.
  • Repetition support: repeat dictated chunks for flexible practice.
  • Multilingual practice, including English and Kannada.
  • Detailed results: word count, total typed words, spelling errors, final WPM and total time taken.
  • Automatic weekly Excel reports with the person's name and practice topic.
  • Missed-day tracking, recorded automatically in the weekly reports.
  • Automatic update checking for new releases.
  • Built for Windows with accessibility and keyboard navigation in mind.

Perfect for

  • Students and competitive-exam preparation
  • Typing practice and dictation training
  • Screen-reader users
  • Anyone who wants to improve typing speed and accuracy

Tip: make sure NVDA is running for speech functionality.
