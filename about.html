About Tech for V.I.

Tech for V.I. is a technology initiative focused on accessibility, independence and digital inclusion. Our goal is to encourage the visually impaired community to explore technology, learn new skills and benefit from useful software.

Our mission

  • Encourage independence through technology.
  • Share useful and accessible software projects.
  • Support screen-reader-friendly computing.
  • Promote learning, creativity and collaboration.
  • Make technology more accessible to everyone.
