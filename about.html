About Tech for V.I.

Tech for V.I. – Accessible Technology for Everyone! Welcome to Tech for V.I., a community dedicated to making technology accessible to everyone.

Our story

Started on April 23, 2019.

Our journey began on April 23, 2019, with something simple: chatting, connecting with one another, and sharing knowledge. In those early days, many of us were still learning how to use mobile phones and navigate the world of technology. Through our conversations, we learned from one another, shared experiences, and grew together.

What started as casual conversations has now grown into a journey of learning, innovation, and development. Today, we are taking steps towards developing accessible technology and empowering people through digital knowledge.

Your support has been an important part of our journey. We are grateful to everyone who has supported us, shared knowledge, and grown with our community over the years.

Our mission

Our mission is to promote accessible technology, share useful knowledge, and make digital tools easier to understand and use for everyone, especially people with visual impairments.

We believe that technology should be inclusive, accessible, and empowering for all.

  • Encourage independence through technology.
  • Share useful and accessible software projects.
  • Support screen-reader-friendly computing.
  • Promote learning, creativity and collaboration.
  • Make technology more accessible to everyone.
